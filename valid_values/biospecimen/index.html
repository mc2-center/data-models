    Valid Value Description Ontology
    Additional - New Primary A biospecimen collected from a new primary malignancy that arose in a patient in addition to a previously diagnosed primary tumor. NCIT:C156441
    Additional Metastatic A biospecimen derived from an additional metastatic tumor site, collected separately from the first-sampled metastatic lesion. NCIT:C156440
    Analyte A substance or chemical constituent extracted or purified from a biospecimen that serves as the target of an analytical procedure, such as a nucleic acid or protein preparation. NCIT:C128639
    Benign Neoplasms A neoplasm that lacks the morphologic features of malignancy, such as severe cytologic atypia or tumor cell necrosis, and remains confined to its site of origin without metastasizing. MONDO:0005165
    Blood Buffy Coat The fraction of an anticoagulated blood sample that contains most of the white blood cells and platelets following density gradient centrifugation of whole blood. BTO:0006181
    Blood Clot A semisolid gelatinous mass of coagulated blood consisting of red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets entrapped in a fibrin network. UBERON:0010210
    Blood Derived Cancer - Bone Marrow A bone marrow specimen collected from a subject that has been diagnosed with a hematologic (blood-derived) malignancy. NCIT:C164015
    Blood Derived Cancer - Bone Marrow; Post-treatment A bone marrow specimen collected from a subject with a hematologic malignancy after the subject has received treatment. NCIT:C164017
    Blood Derived Cancer - Peripheral Blood A peripheral whole blood specimen collected from a subject that has been diagnosed with a hematologic (blood-derived) malignancy. NCIT:C164030
    Blood Derived Cancer - Peripheral Blood; Post-treatment A peripheral whole blood specimen collected from a subject with a hematologic malignancy after the subject has received treatment. NCIT:C164031
    Blood Derived Liquid Biopsy A blood-based liquid biopsy specimen used to sample circulating tumor cells, cell-free nucleic acids, or other tumor-derived material without a solid tissue biopsy. NCIT:C135727
    Blood Derived Normal A peripheral blood specimen collected from a subject that is used as a source of germline (non-tumor) DNA or as a matched normal control for genomic comparisons. NCIT:C17610
    Body Fluid A liquid substance derived from the body, such as blood, urine, cerebrospinal fluid, or pleural effusion, that is collected as a biospecimen. EMAPA:37441
    Bone Marrow Normal A bone marrow specimen collected from a subject that does not contain malignant cells and is used as a normal reference sample. NCIT:C164009
    Buccal Cell Normal A sample of normal (non-tumor) cells collected by scraping or swabbing the inside of a subject's cheek, commonly used as a source of germline DNA. NCIT:C172264
    Cell The smallest unit of living structure capable of independent existence, composed of a membrane-enclosed mass of protoplasm containing a nucleus or nucleoid. NCIT:C12508
    Cell Line Derived Xenograft Tissue A biospecimen derived from tissue that developed after a laboratory cell line was implanted and grown in a non-human host organism. NCIT:C156443
    Cell Lines A permanently established cell culture that will proliferate indefinitely given appropriate fresh medium and growth space. NCIT:C16403
    Control Analyte A biospecimen or analyte that is used as a comparator or reference against an experimental sample. NCIT:C156442
    DNA A long, linear, double-stranded polymer of nucleotides attached to a deoxyribose backbone that carries an organism's genetic information; used here to denote a specimen consisting of extracted DNA. NCIT:C449
    EBV Immortalized Normal A biospecimen consisting of normal lymphocytes that have been infected in vitro with Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) and cultured to establish an indefinitely proliferating cell line. NCIT:C163993
    Expanded Next Generation Cancer Model A next generation cancer model that has been expanded using tissue culture or other propagation methods. NCIT:C184757
    FFPE Recurrent A formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tissue sample derived from tissue isolated from a subject with recurrent disease. NCIT:C172267
    FFPE Scrolls A formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tissue sample that has been cut on a microtome into a thin section that rolls up on itself, forming a scroll. NCIT:C172268
    Fibroblasts from Bone Marrow Normal Fibroblast cells isolated from a normal (non-malignant) bone marrow specimen. NCIT:C12482
    GenomePlex (Rubicon) Amplified DNA A DNA specimen that has been amplified using the GenomePlex (Rubicon Genomics) whole genome amplification method. NCIT:C198569
    Granulocytes A type of leukocyte with a multilobed nucleus and cytoplasmic granules, subdivided into eosinophils, basophils, and neutrophils based on the staining properties of their granules. NCIT:C12530
    Human Tumor Original Cells A sample comprised of cells that were isolated from a human tumor and have not been cultured or expanded. NCIT:C172293
    In Situ Neoplasms Neoplasms confined to the site of origin without invasion beyond the basement membrane or into surrounding tissue, such as carcinoma in situ. NCIT:C36118
    Lymphoid Normal A specimen of normal (non-malignant) lymphoid tissue or cells used as a reference or control sample. NCIT:C13049
    Metastatic A term referring to the clinical or pathologic observation of a tumor extension from its original site of growth to another anatomic site. NCIT:C14174
    Mixed Adherent Suspension A sample comprised of a mixture of cells or clumps of cells, which may have been isolated from normal or tumor tissue, that are maintained together in a suspension culture. NCIT:C174118
    Mononuclear Cells from Bone Marrow Normal A biological sample containing mononuclear cells isolated from a normal (non-malignant) bone marrow specimen. NCIT:C164011
    Neoplasms of Uncertain and Unknown Behavior Neoplasms that, on morphologic grounds, cannot be classified with certainty as benign or malignant. NCIT:C65157
    Next Generation Cancer Model An approach to cancer modeling that utilizes clinical data as well as the genomic and transcriptomic profiles of the parent tumor, metastases, if present, and case-matched normal tissues from individual patients.NCIT:C172295
    Next Generation Cancer Model Expanded Under Non-conforming ConditionsA next generation cancer model that has been expanded using unconventional or non-standard culturing methods. NCIT:C184781
    Not Allowed To Collect An indicator that specifies that a biospecimen collection event was not permitted. NCIT:C141478
    Pleural Effusion Increased amounts of fluid within the pleural cavity, usually caused by lung infections, congestive heart failure, pleural or lung tumors, connective tissue disorders, or trauma. NCIT:C3331
    Post neo-adjuvant therapy A biospecimen collected from a subject after treatment with a neoadjuvant therapeutic agent. NCIT:C156444
    Primary Blood Derived Cancer - Bone Marrow A bone marrow specimen collected from a subject at the time of initial diagnosis of a hematologic (blood-derived) malignancy, prior to treatment. NCIT:C164015
    Primary Blood Derived Cancer - Peripheral Blood A peripheral whole blood specimen collected from a subject at the time of initial diagnosis of a hematologic malignancy, prior to treatment. NCIT:C164030
    Primary Tumor A tumor specimen collected from the anatomic site where the cancer originated, as opposed to a metastatic or recurrent site. NCIT:C8509
    Primary Xenograft Tissue Tissue derived from a patient's tumor that has been directly implanted and grown in a non-human host organism without prior in vitro culturing. NCIT:C122936
    Recurrent Blood Derived Cancer - Bone Marrow A bone marrow specimen collected from a subject at the time of relapse or recurrence of a hematologic malignancy. NCIT:C164017
    Recurrent Blood Derived Cancer - Peripheral Blood A peripheral whole blood specimen collected from a subject at the time of relapse or recurrence of a hematologic malignancy. NCIT:C164031
    Recurrent Tumor A tumor specimen collected after the reemergence of cancer at the original or a related site following a period of remission. NCIT:C4798
    Repli-G (Qiagen) DNA A genomic DNA specimen that has been amplified from a limited-quantity sample using the REPLI-g (Qiagen) whole genome amplification technology. NCIT:C156437
    Repli-G X (Qiagen) DNA A genomic DNA specimen amplified from an ultra-low-input sample using the REPLI-g X (Qiagen) whole genome amplification kit, designed for single-cell or trace-quantity starting material. NCIT:C156439
    RNA A biospecimen created to contain an isolated or enriched ribonucleic acid (RNA) sample. NCIT:C198568
    Saliva The watery fluid in the mouth produced by the salivary glands that moistens food to aid digestion and helps protect the mouth against infection. NCIT:C13275
    Slides A biospecimen consisting of a tissue section or cell preparation mounted on a glass slide for microscopic examination. NCIT:C62755
    Solid Tissue Normal A specimen of morphologically normal solid tissue collected from a subject, typically used as a matched normal control for a tumor specimen. NCIT:C164014
    Tissue An anatomical structure consisting of similarly specialized cells and intercellular matrix, aggregated according to genetically determined spatial relationships, that performs a specific function. NCIT:C12801
    Total RNA A biological sample comprised of all of the RNA collected from an experimental subject. NCIT:C163995
    Tumor An abnormal mass of tissue that results from excessive cell division; used broadly to denote a neoplasm, which may be benign or malignant. NCIT:C3262
    Tumor Adjacent Normal - Post Neo-adjuvant Therapy A specimen of morphologically normal tissue collected from the area immediately surrounding a tumor, obtained from a subject after treatment with a neoadjuvant therapeutic. NCIT:C164033
    Whole Animal Referring to the entire body of an experimental animal; used when a biospecimen consists of or is derived from an entire organism. NCIT:C77665
    Whole Blood Blood that has not been separated into its various components, other than the addition of an anticoagulant. NCIT:C41067
    Xenograft Tissue Tissue derived from human or animal cells or tumor material that has been transplanted into and grown within a different (non-human) host organism. NCIT:C122936
    Unknown Not known, observed, recorded; or reported as unknown by the data contributor. NCIT:C17998
    Not Reported Not provided or available. NCIT:C43234
