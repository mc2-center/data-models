    Attribute: Grant Type¶

    Valid Value Description Ontology
    R01 NIH Research Project Grant: supports a discrete, specified, circumscribed research project in an area representing the specific interests and competencies of the investigator(s). Not available
    R21 NIH Exploratory/Developmental Research Grant: supports the early and conceptual stages of project development, encouraging new, exploratory, and high-risk/high-reward research. Not available
    R37 NIH Method to Extend Research in Time (MERIT) Award: extended, non-competing funding provided to investigators with an R01 who have demonstrated superior competence and productivity.Not available
    U01 NIH Research Project Cooperative Agreement: supports a discrete, specified research project in which substantial programmatic involvement by NIH staff is anticipated. Not available
    U24 NIH Resource-Related Research Projects Cooperative Agreement: supports research and development of resources that will enhance the capability of researchers to conduct research. Not available
    U54 NIH Specialized Center Cooperative Agreement: supports a multi-project, multi-disciplinary research center with substantial NIH programmatic involvement. Not available

    Attribute: Grant Theme Name¶

    Valid Value Description Ontology
    Computational Model DevelopmentResearch theme focused on building computational or mathematical models to simulate, predict, or explain biological/cancer-related processes. Not available
    Computational Resource Research theme focused on developing software tools, databases, or computational infrastructure for the cancer research community. Not available
    Drug Resistance/Sensitivity Mechanisms, functions, activities, or processes exhibited by tumors or pathogens to withstand previously toxic drugs. NCIT:C16523
    Epigenetics Topic concerning the study of heritable changes, for example in gene expression or phenotype, caused by mechanisms other than changes in the DNA sequence. EDAM:topic_3295
    Evolution The process by which organism populations change over successive generations that is influenced by gene mutation and natural selection. NCIT:C16565
    Experimental Model Development Research theme focused on creating or characterizing new experimental (wet-lab) model systems, such as cell lines, organoids, or animal models. Not available
    Heterogeneity The quality or state of consisting of elements that are not of the same kind or nature. NCIT:C28359
    Immunotherapy Therapy designed to induce changes in a patient's immune status in order to treat disease. NCIT:C15262
    Mechano-genetics Research theme focused on how mechanical forces influence gene expression and genetic regulation within cells and tissues. Not available
    Mechano-resistance Research theme focused on how mechanical properties of cells or tissues contribute to resistance against therapies or disease progression. Not available
    Metabolism Organic processes in a cell or organism that are necessary to sustain life, encompassing both anabolism (construction of complex molecules) and catabolism (breakdown of complex substances). NCIT:C19536
    Metastasis The spread or migration of cancer cells from one part of the body (the organ in which it first appeared) to another. The secondary tumor contains cells that are like those in the original (primary) tumor. NCIT:C19151
    Method/Assay Development Research theme focused on creating or improving experimental methods, assays, or protocols. Not available
    Microenvironment The cellular and molecular environment immediately surrounding a tumor, including blood vessels, immune cells, fibroblasts, signaling molecules, and extracellular matrix, that interacts with and influences tumor growth and progression.NCIT:C94498
    Oncogenic Stress Research theme focused on cellular stress responses induced by oncogenic events, such as DNA damage response or replication stress. Not available
    Platform Development Research theme focused on developing new experimental or technological platforms for cancer research. Not available
    Tumor Progression A pathologic process in which alterations at the molecular level result in a more aggressive cytologic and phenotypic profile and clinical course of a neoplasm. NCIT:C17609
    Tumor-Immune Interactions of the immune system and tumor cells. NCIT:C19095
