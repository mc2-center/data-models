    Attribute: Resource Topic¶

    Valid Value Description Ontology
    Computational Model DevelopmentThe construction and refinement of mathematical, statistical, or computational models used to simulate, predict, or analyze biological or clinical phenomena. Not available
    Computational Resource Software tools, algorithms, databases, or computing infrastructure developed or used to support computational research and data analysis. Not available
    Diversity/Equity/Inclusion Efforts and practices that promote fair representation, equal opportunity, and inclusion of individuals from diverse backgrounds, identities, and abilities within research and educational communities. MESH:D000092783
    Drug Resistance/Sensitivity The diminished or absent response of cancer cells or tumors to a therapeutic agent (resistance), or their degree of susceptibility to being affected or killed by that agent (sensitivity). Not available
    Epigenetics The study of heritable changes in gene expression or cellular phenotype caused by mechanisms other than alterations in the underlying DNA sequence, such as DNA methylation and histone modification. EDAM:3295
    Evolution The process by which populations of organisms change over successive generations through mechanisms such as mutation, genetic drift, and natural selection. NCIT:C16565
    Experimental Model Development The design, generation, and validation of laboratory-based systems, such as cell lines, organoids, or animal models, used to study disease biology or evaluate therapeutic interventions. Not available
    Heterogeneity The quality or state of consisting of elements that are not of the same kind or nature, such as variability among cells, tissues, or tumors within a biological system. NCIT:C28359
    Immunotherapy Therapy designed to induce, enhance, or suppress changes in a patient's immune status in order to treat disease, such as cancer. NCIT:C15262
    Mechano-genetics The study of how mechanical forces and the physical properties of cells and tissues influence gene expression and genetic regulation. Not available
    Mechano-resistance The study of how mechanical properties of, or forces acting on, cells and tissues contribute to resistance against therapeutic agents or other stressors. Not available
    Metabolism The organic biochemical processes within a cell or organism that are necessary to sustain life, encompassing both anabolic (biosynthetic) and catabolic (degradative) reactions. NCIT:C19536
    Metastasis The spread of cancer cells from the primary tumor site to other parts of the body, forming secondary tumors that share characteristics of the original neoplasm. NCIT:C19151
    Method/Assay Development The design, optimization, and validation of new experimental techniques, protocols, or assays for measuring biological or clinical phenomena. Not available
    Microenvironment The cellular and molecular environment surrounding a tumor or biological system, including neighboring cells, blood vessels, immune cells, signaling molecules, and the extracellular matrix, that influences its growth and behavior. NCIT:C94498
    Oncogenic Stress Cellular stress arising from the activation of oncogenes or dysregulated growth signaling, which can trigger protective responses such as senescence or apoptosis. Not available
    Other A resource topic that does not fit within any of the other defined categories. Not available
    Outreach Activities and programs designed to engage, inform, or provide resources to communities, patients, or the public outside of a research institution's core scientific work. Not available
    Platform Development The creation and refinement of technological, computational, or experimental platforms and infrastructure that support research workflows or data generation. Not available
    Training Material A document or other resource, such as a tutorial, course, or teaching module, designed for learning, educational, or training purposes. EDAM:3669
    Tumor Progression A pathologic process in which molecular alterations result in a more aggressive cytologic and phenotypic profile and clinical course of a neoplasm over time. NCIT:C17609
    Tumor-Immune The study of the interactions between the immune system and tumor cells, including immune surveillance, immune evasion, and immune-based responses to cancer. NCIT:C19095
    Computer Science The study of the structure, theory, and application of computational systems, algorithms, and information processing using mathematical, logical, and empirical methods. NCIT:C18141
    Environmental Science The interdisciplinary scientific study of external biotic and abiotic factors to which an organism is exposed, and their effects on health and ecosystems. MESH:D000079265
    Information Science The field of knowledge, theory, and technology concerned with the collection, organization, storage, retrieval, and dissemination of facts and data, including aspects of communication, publishing, and informatics. MESH:D007254
    Educational Technology The systematic identification, development, organization, and use of educational resources, tools, and equipment, including audiovisual and digital aids, to support teaching and learning. MESH:D018961
    Anatomy/Physiology The study of the structure of living organisms and their parts (anatomy) together with the functions and mechanisms of those structures (physiology). Not available
    Biology The scientific study of living organisms, including their structure, function, growth, evolution, and interactions with their environment. NCIT:C16345
    Ecology The branch of biology concerned with the relationships between organisms and their environment. NCIT:C16526
    Genetics The branch of biology that deals with heredity, especially the mechanisms of hereditary transmission and the variation of inherited characteristics among similar or related organisms. NCIT:C16624
    Nutrition The processes by which an organism consumes and utilizes food, water, and other nourishment necessary to sustain its life processes. NCIT:C28294
    Measurement and Data An educational topic area covering skills and concepts related to measuring quantities and to collecting, representing, and interpreting data. Not available
    Statistics and Probability The application of statistical methods and probability theory to describe, summarize, and draw inferences from data. EDAM:2269
    Chemistry The scientific study of the composition, structure, properties, and reactions of matter, especially atomic and molecular systems. NCIT:C16414
    Physics The scientific study of matter, energy, and the fundamental forces and laws governing their interactions and behavior in nature. NCIT:C16989
    Systems Biology An interdisciplinary field that studies complex interactions within biological systems using a holistic approach, integrating computational and mathematical modeling with experimental data to understand emergent properties of the system as a whole.OMIT:0024179
    Patient Advocacy Efforts to promote, protect, and represent the rights, needs, and interests of patients, often through education, support services, or engagement in research and health policy processes. MESH:D010344

    Attribute: Resource Activity Type¶

    Valid Value Description Ontology
    Activity/Lab A hands-on exercise or laboratory-based activity in which learners actively perform tasks, experiments, or procedures to apply and reinforce concepts. Not available
    Assessment A resource used to evaluate learner understanding or performance, such as a quiz, test, or other measurement of academic achievement. MESH:D004521
    Case Study A detailed, narrative-based educational resource examining a specific real-world example, scenario, or problem to illustrate concepts or promote analytical and critical-thinking skills. Not available
    Data Set A structured collection of related data records provided as an educational resource for analysis, exploration, or hands-on learning exercises. NCIT:C47824
    Diagram/Illustration A visual representation, such as a diagram, chart, or illustration, used to depict concepts, structures, or processes for instructional purposes. EVORAO:Image
    Full Course A complete, self-contained sequence of instructional materials and activities covering a full subject or curriculum, typically spanning a full academic term. Not available
    Game An interactive, rule-based activity designed to engage learners and teach or reinforce concepts through play. Not available
    Homework/Assignment Work or tasks assigned to learners for completion outside of the primary instructional setting, used to reinforce or extend learning. NCIT:C89269
    Interactive A resource that requires active learner participation or response, such as a clickable simulation, interactive exercise, or dynamic multimedia element. Not available
    Lecture A recorded or transcribed instructional talk delivered to an audience or class, typically used to present or explain subject matter. MESH:D019531
    Lecture Notes Written notes, outlines, or summaries prepared for or derived from a lecture, used to supplement or review instructional content. MESH:D019528
    Lesson A single, discrete unit of instruction focused on a specific topic or set of learning objectives. Not available
    Lesson Plan A structured outline prepared by an instructor detailing the objectives, activities, materials, and assessment methods for a lesson. Not available
    Module A self-contained segment of instructional content covering a specific topic, often combined with other modules to form a larger course or curriculum. Not available
    Primary Source An original document, artifact, or firsthand record (such as a letter, dataset, or historical artifact) created at the time of the event or phenomenon under study, used as direct evidence for learning or research.Not available
    Reading A text-based resource, such as an article, excerpt, or book chapter, assigned for learners to read as part of instructional content. Not available
    Simulation An interactive model or representation of a real-world process, system, or scenario that allows learners to explore and experiment in a controlled, simulated environment. Not available
    Student Guide A resource providing learners with instructions, guidance, or supplementary information to support their use of a course or activity. Not available
    Syllabus A document describing the material, structure, objectives, and requirements covered in a course. schema:Syllabus
    Teaching/Learning StrategyAn instructional method, technique, or procedure used by educators to facilitate teaching or by learners to facilitate learning of new material. OCCO:00000030
    Textbook A book intended for use in the study of a specific subject, providing a systematic presentation of its principles and essential knowledge. MESH:D022923
    Unit of Study A cohesive set of lessons or learning materials organized around a specific topic or theme, typically spanning several class sessions. Not available

    Attribute: Resource Media Accessibility¶

    Valid Value Description Ontology
    Alternative Text A text alternative provided for non-text content (such as images), allowing assistive technologies like screen readers to convey the content's meaning to users who cannot see it. Not available
    Audio Description An additional narration track that describes important visual information in video content (actions, scenes, on-screen text) for users who are blind or have low vision. Not available
    Braille Indicates the resource, or a version of it, is available in Braille, a tactile writing system that enables reading by touch for people who are blind or have low vision. Not available
    Captions A synchronized, on-screen text display of spoken dialogue and other audio content within a video, providing an optional transcript of the audio for viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing. NCIT:C185118
    ChemML An XML-based markup format (analogous to Chemical Markup Language) used to encode chemical formulas and notation in an accessible, machine-readable form for assistive technologies. EDAM:format_4023
    Described Math A text-based or audio description of mathematical notation and expressions, provided as an alternative to visual math formulas for users who use screen readers or other assistive technology. Not available
    Display TransformabilityIndicates that the resource's visual presentation (e.g., text size, color, spacing, layout) can be modified or resized by the user without loss of content or functionality, supporting users with low vision or reading disabilities.Not available
    Haptic Indicates that the resource provides touch-based (haptic) feedback, such as vibration, force, or tactile cues, as an alternative or supplement to visual or auditory information. MESH:D000089862
    High Contrast Indicates that a high-contrast display option (e.g., strongly differentiated foreground/background colors) is available to improve readability for users with low vision. Not available
    Large Print Indicates that the resource, or a version of it, is available in a large-print format with enlarged text to improve readability for users with low vision. Not available
    Latex Indicates that mathematical or scientific content is encoded using LaTeX, a document preparation and markup system, allowing accessible rendering of complex notation. SWO:3000025
    Long Description A detailed textual description provided for complex non-text content (such as charts, graphs, or images) that cannot be adequately conveyed through brief alternative text alone. Not available
    MathML An XML-based markup language for describing mathematical notation and expressions, capturing both structure and content to enable accessible rendering of formulas in web documents. NCIT:C71609
    Nemeth Braille A specialized Braille code used to represent mathematical and scientific notation, enabling readers who are blind or have low vision to access complex math content. Not available
    Sign Language A system of hand gestures, facial expressions, and body movements used for communication, provided here as a video-based interpretation of content for users who are deaf or hard of hearing. NCIT:C51278
    Structural Navigation Indicates that the resource is structured with navigational markup (such as headings, landmarks, or lists) that allows assistive technology users to move efficiently between sections of content. Not available
    Tactile Graphics Raised or textured versions of images, diagrams, or graphics that can be explored by touch, enabling users who are blind or have low vision to perceive visual information. Not available
    Text Transcript A full text version of the spoken and relevant non-speech audio content of a media resource, allowing users to read the content instead of, or in addition to, listening to it. Not available

    Attribute: Resource Access Hazard¶

    Valid Value Description Ontology
    Flashing Indicates the educational resource contains flashing or strobing visual effects that may pose a seizure risk for photosensitive users. Not available
    Motion Indicates the educational resource contains motion effects (e.g., panning, zooming, or animated movement) that may cause discomfort or disorientation for motion-sensitive users.Not available
    Simulation Indicates the educational resource contains simulated or virtual environments that may pose a hazard for users sensitive to simulated motion or visual effects. Not available
    Sound Indicates the educational resource contains audio content, including unexpected or startling sounds, that may pose a hazard for users with sound sensitivities. Not available
